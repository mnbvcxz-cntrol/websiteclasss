## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a luxury, tactile, and editorial commerce experience rooted in craftsmanship and thoughtful curation. Designed for discerning patrons seeking bespoke, handmade treasures and intentional gifting, the aesthetic marries traditional bespoke ateliers with contemporary high-end publishing.

The design movement balances **Minimalist Luxury** with **Tactile Warmth**. It eschews sterile, hyper-flat tech minimalism in favor of textured serenity, organic warmth, and literary gravity. 

### Core Design Tenets
- **Curatorial Restraint:** Expansive negative space acts as a pedestal for physical objects. Visual density is intentionally suppressed to spotlight materials, grain, and provenance.
- **Editorial Poise:** High-contrast serif headlines evoke luxury printed journals, contrasted against functional, clinical sans-serif metadata.
- **Tactile Softness:** Subdued hairline dividers, linen-like backdrop tones, and whispering shadows evoke raw parchment, uncoated packaging boxes, and ribbon finishes.

## Colors

The palette references raw ceramic, unbleached cotton, pressed paper, and rich wood stains. The interface remains resolutely anchored in a warm light canvas.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#342820` — Deep Espresso):** The bedrock of readability. Used for crisp typographic hierarchy, high-contrast actions, key iconography, and anchor branding elements. It replaces generic carbon blacks with deep roasted richness.
- **Secondary (`#D8C6AD` — Champagne Beige):** The tactile bridge. Applied to subtle borders, premium accent highlights, active indicators, and secondary tactile surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#A7AD99` — Muted Sage):** Botanical and restorative. Reserved for subtle status badges ('Bestseller', 'In Stock'), sustainability callouts, and seasonal curation tags.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F5F0` — Warm Ivory):** The atmospheric base across page shells, framing photography without cold screen glare.

### Supporting Canvas Tones
- **Soft Cream (`#EFE7DC`):** Surface containers, modal panels, filter drawers, and card backdrops.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity media frames, input fields, and pristine checkout surfaces.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial tension between romantic, literary craftsmanship and modernist architectural precision.

### Typographic Hierarchy
- **Editorial Headings (`Playfair Display`):** Reserved for product narratives, artisanal stories, collection titles, and primary category anchors. Set with loose, elegant line-heights and slight negative tracking on large scales to retain a classical print identity.
- **Utility & Structure (`Inter`):** Deployed for all product metadata, navigational items, specifications, checkout inputs, and currency pricing. Set with neutral metrics to guarantee legibility and structured precision.
- **Pricing & Currency:** Currency values (PKR) must always render in tabular sans-serif numerals with consistent spacing (`Rs.` prefix, spaced: `Rs. 18,500`), preserving clean visual baselines against serif titles.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Badges, uppercase meta tags, and categories utilize expanded letter-spacing (0.08em–0.1em) for typographic breathing room.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model adheres to an asymmetric, airy grid system inspired by editorial art books and luxury lookbooks.

### Layout Philosophy
- **Responsive Fluid Grid:** 12-column layout on desktop (`> 1024px`) with dynamic outer margins up to `max-width: 1440px`. Mobile screens compress to a 4-column system with compact gutters and edge padding.
- **Intentional Negative Space:** Generous vertical rhythm separates narrative story modules from commercial catalog strips. Breathing room takes precedence over high visual density.
- **Asymmetric Offsets:** Product collections interleave standard multi-column grids with full-bleed editorial imagery, single-item highlights, and bespoke artisan quote spans.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this system is organic and physical rather than digital. Surfaces mimic stacked paper stock, linen mats, and soft natural light.

### Tonal Stratification & Borders
- **Hairline Framing:** Separation is primarily driven by 1px crisp borders using Champagne Beige (`#D8C6AD`) at full or 60% opacity. Avoid heavy borders or stark dividers.
- **Tonal Layers:** The ground canvas starts at Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F0`). Interactive modules, drawers, and flyouts sit on Soft Cream (`#EFE7DC`), and product showcases sit within crisp Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) pods.

### Ambient Shadows
- **Level 1 (Card & Subtle Lift):** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(52, 40, 32, 0.04)`. Extremely diffused, tinted by Deep Espresso to simulate gentle, indirect afternoon room lighting.
- **Level 2 (Popovers & Flyouts):** `0 12px 36px -4px rgba(52, 40, 32, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Cart Drawer):** `0 24px 64px -8px rgba(52, 40, 32, 0.12)`, paired with an atmospheric backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)` over `rgba(52, 40, 32, 0.25)`).

## Shapes

The shape system leverages subtle, tailored corners (`Soft: 0.25rem`) that honor the precision of handcrafted bookbinding and artisanal box packaging. Completely sharp corners feel overly clinical, while large pill shapes erode the editorial luxury posture.

### Corner Radius System
- **Base (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to buttons, standard text fields, product cards, status badges, and dropdown menus.
- **Medium / `rounded-lg` (0.5rem / 8px):** Dialog windows, gift personalization preview drawers, and oversized banner frames.
- **Circular (Full):** Restricted exclusively to icon-only buttons (e.g., wishlist heart toggle, carousel navigation arrows) and color/material swatch pickers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Deep Espresso (`#342820`) background with Warm Ivory (`#F8F5F0`) typography. `rounded: 0.25rem`, padding `0.875rem 2rem`. Subtle lift and micro-opacity transition (`0.92`) on hover.
- **Secondary Action:** Transparent background with 1px solid Deep Espresso border, filled with Soft Cream on hover.
- **Artisan / Text Button:** Deep Espresso text with an understated 1px underline positioned with a 4px offset. Underline smoothly expands from center on hover.

### Elevated Product Cards
- **Structure:** Clean Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) or Warm Ivory surface container with a 1px `#D8C6AD` perimeter border.
- **Image Container:** 3:4 aspect ratio with an off-white background wash. Images feature an ultra-smooth, slow zoom transform (`scale(1.04)`) over `600ms cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)` on hover.
- **Metadata Block:** Bottom section aligned with generous padding (`space-md`), featuring product category in `label-sm`, title in `headline-sm`, and pricing displayed clearly in PKR format (`Rs. 24,000`).

### Badges & Tags
- **'Bestseller' / 'Curator’s Choice':** Soft Cream (`#EFE7DC`) fill with Deep Espresso text, enclosed in a fine Champagne Beige hairline border.
- **'Made to Order' / 'Personalized':** Pale Sage tint (`#A7AD99` at 15% opacity) with Deep Espresso text.
- **Styling:** Uppercase, `label-sm`, tracking `0.1em`, padding `0.25rem 0.625rem`, `rounded: 0.25rem`.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- **Form Controls:** Uncluttered white field resting on soft cream background with 1px border `#D8C6AD`. Focused state transitions border to `#342820` without abrupt glowing outlines.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted minimalist squares and circles with a 1px `#342820` stroke and subtle espresso fill when selected.

### Product Detail & Personalization Modules
- **Gift Monogram & Personalization Panel:** Nested container in `#EFE7DC` with live serif typography preview. Includes character counter and custom notes toggle with linen-textured separator lines.
- **Currency & Pricing Display:** PKR formatting prioritized everywhere (`Rs. 12,500`), accompanied by transparent tax and artisanal packaging notices.
